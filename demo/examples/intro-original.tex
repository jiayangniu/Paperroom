\section*{Introduction}
Each exact evaluation can dominate the cost of a search. Screening offers a simple alternative: score the candidates cheaply and reserve exact evaluations for those that appear most promising.

We compare a screening rule with an unscreened search in three paired runs. We record how many exact evaluations each method uses. The aim is to determine whether screening reduces this cost.
